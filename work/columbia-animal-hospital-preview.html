What we do

Everything your pet needs, in one place in Columbia.

Surgery

A state-of-the-art surgical suite equipped with innovative tools, and veterinarians committed to the highest level of care available to your furry friends.

Digital Radiography

Our digital radiography system diagnoses many conditions quicker and more accurately than older imaging — so your pet gets the care they need sooner.

Grooming

Our experienced groomers have kept Columbia and Hattiesburg area pets looking and feeling their best for many years. Call to schedule an appointment.

Wellness & Prevention

Annual exams, vaccinations and parasite prevention that keep small problems small — plus a veterinarian on call after hours.
